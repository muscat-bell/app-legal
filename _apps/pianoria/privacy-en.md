# Privacy Policy

> This English version is a translation of the original Japanese Privacy Policy, provided for your convenience. In the event of any inconsistency or discrepancy between the English version and the Japanese version, the Japanese version shall prevail.

## 1. Introduction

Pianoria (the "App") is a piano practice support application provided by Muscat Bell Labs ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how personal information and other data of users of the App ("you" or "users") are handled.

By using the App, you are deemed to have agreed to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Direct Collection of Personal Information

The App does not directly collect personal information from you, such as your name, email address, postal address, or phone number.

### 2.2 User Registration

The App has no user registration feature. You can use the App without creating an account.

### 2.3 Information Collected by Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to deliver advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect the following information to display ads and measure their effectiveness.

**Information that may be collected:**
- Device information (device model, OS version, screen size, etc.)
- Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
- IP address
- App usage information
- Ad view and click information

**About the advertising identifier:**
- On Android devices, the "Google Advertising ID" is used
- You can reset or disable this identifier in your device settings
- How to change it: Open "Settings" → "Google" → "Ads," then select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (menu names may vary depending on your device and OS version)

**Google AdMob privacy policy:**
For details, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 2.4 Information Related to In-App Purchases

The App offers in-app purchases through Google Play Billing in order to provide premium features such as ad removal.

**About payment processing:**
- Payments are processed through the Google Play Store
- Payment information (such as credit card details) is managed by the Google Play Store; we do not directly obtain or store it
- Your purchase history is stored in association with your Google Play account

**Storage of purchase status:**
- Only information on whether you have made a purchase is stored on your device, in encrypted form
- This information is used solely to control the display of ads

**Google Play Billing privacy policy:**
For details, please refer to the Google Play Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
https://play.google.com/about/privacy-security-deception/

### 2.5 Analytics

The App does not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

### 2.6 Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability. If the App crashes, the following information is sent automatically.

**Information collected:**
- Crash logs (where the error occurred, stack traces)
- Device information (device model, OS version)
- App version

**Purpose of collection:**
- To identify and fix bugs in the App

This information is anonymized and does not include information that identifies you personally, such as your name or email address.

**Firebase Crashlytics privacy policy:**
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 3. Data Storage and Management

### 3.1 Local Storage

The App stores the following information only on your device.

**Information stored:**
- App settings (piano roll design, keyboard color settings, etc.)
- Purchase status (whether you have purchased the ad removal feature)
- Imported music files (MIDI files, MusicXML files) and sound sample files
- Music library organization data (folder structure, favorites, playback/viewing history, A-B repeat positions, etc.)
- Fingering information you have entered (fingering notes)

All of this information is created or imported by you within the App, and we do not transmit it externally or obtain it.

**Storage location:**
- All data is stored in an area of your device dedicated to the App
- It is stored in a sandboxed area that cannot be accessed by other apps

### 3.2 Cloud Synchronization

The App does not provide any feature that automatically sends or synchronizes your data to servers operated by us or by third parties. In principle, the data described above is stored only on your device.

However, this does not apply if you export data externally using the "Backup and Restore" feature described below.

### 3.3 Backup and Restore

The App provides a feature that exports in-app data, such as settings, folders, music, and fingering notes, into a single backup file (file extension `.pnrbak`) that can be restored later.

**About export destinations:**
- A backup is exported only when you explicitly perform the export operation
- The export uses your device's (OS) sharing feature, and you choose the destination yourself (Google Drive, iCloud Drive, files on your device, other supported apps, etc.)
- If you choose a cloud service such as Google Drive or iCloud as the destination, the backup file is stored on that service through your own account. In this case, the handling of that data is governed by the privacy policy and terms of service of the respective service
- We do not obtain, store, or view the contents of exported backup files

**About protection of backup files:**
- Backup files are protected with simple encryption (a password-protected ZIP)
- This is only light protection and does not guarantee strong confidentiality. Because backup files contain your in-app data, please manage the storage destination appropriately at your own responsibility

**About restoring:**
- A restore is performed by loading a backup file that you select
- When you restore, your current in-app data is overwritten with the contents of the backup

### 3.4 Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored in the area of your device dedicated to the App.

However, backup files that you have exported externally (to a cloud service, another location on your device, etc.) using the "Backup and Restore" feature are not automatically deleted when you uninstall the App. Please delete those files yourself.
